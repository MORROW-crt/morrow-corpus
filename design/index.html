02 / Specific visual families

Fetish–space–time: three elements in tension.

The restricted philosophical proposal informs a specific motif family: apparent autonomy of an object or category; the spatial organization of colonial and postcolonial relations; and historical layers through which that appearance is reproduced or transformed.

For the historical Obeah inquiry, a colonial category, enforcement geography and the period of its later transformations should appear as distinguishable layers. The site must never invent sacred iconography or treat generated stamps and maps as genuine records.
