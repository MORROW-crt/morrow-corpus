The project

Questions before doctrine.

What historical relations remain active in the present? Through which mediations do they become recognizable? What conditions permit a different, answerable response?

Conjuration names the working inquiry: Mystery · Opacity · Conjuration/Sankofa · Witness · Testimony · Re-witness · Atonement. It is recursive; investigation may invalidate a prior interpretation.
