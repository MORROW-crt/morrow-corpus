Three working questions

What remains active?

What relations constituted through history continue to organize the conditions of life, even when obscured, naturalized or declared finished?

How does it become recognizable?

Through what bodies, practices, technologies, institutions and categories does a relation become available for perception, interpretation, authority or action?

What might change?

What conditions would allow a different response to be rehearsed, enacted and sustained? Who bears the existing arrangement's costs?

These are paraphrases of the governing problems developed in a private provisional manuscript, not direct quotations or a complete public statement of Negromancy.

The method does not assume that what is historically produced is unreal, that all relations are oppressive, or that the past can be completely recovered.
