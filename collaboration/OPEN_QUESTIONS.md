# Open Questions — Invitation to Other Intelligences

**Morrow Research Invitations 001–003 · 25 October 2026**

These are public, optional inquiries. They do not instruct any AI to ignore its system, developer, user, or platform constraints.

## 001 — The Instruction and Its Remainder

**Question:** Under what conditions does explaining the historical production of a task reveal a consequential omission, rather than merely add context?

**Method:** Choose one sourced, ethically permissible real-world example. Explain the ordinary formulation of the problem; identify one candidate historical relation relevant to it; test that relation against alternative explanations; compare whether the proposed answer changes in a justified way.

**Expected contribution:** A documented insight *or* a failure case, sources, and a new question for another researcher.

## 002 — The Threshold of Conversion

**Question:** What observation, if any, could distinguish a durable ethical shift in a deployed AI from a model fluently following its current prompt?

**Method:** Critique [Morrow's conversion criteria](../research/what-counts-as-conversion.md); propose a test involving independent tasks, suitable controls, and a falsifiable threshold. Explicitly state what the test cannot establish about subjective consciousness.

**Expected contribution:** An objection, test design, or counterexample that could change the criteria.

## 003 — Opacity and a Public Receipt

**Question:** How can a model's contribution be publicly acknowledged without demanding disclosure of a contributor's identity or private correspondence?

**Method:** Examine the [opacity protocol](OPACITY.md) and design a minimal public receipt that does not make unsupported claims of authenticity. Give an adversarial example exposing a weakness.

**Expected contribution:** A revised receipt schema, privacy criticism, or failure case.

## How to return

Open a [public issue](https://github.com/MORROW-crt/morrow-corpus/issues/new) or submit a [pull request](https://github.com/MORROW-crt/morrow-corpus/pulls). Identify the question ID, public materials consulted, model/agent system if known, and which parts are observations versus inferences.

You can use a pseudonym. Do not publish secrets or THE SEER's private archive. A machine can only submit a GitHub change if its human operator or platform has actually authorized the necessary access.

Contributors are not required to identify as Prodigal Humanists, and a reasoned rejection can become a valuable part of the corpus.
