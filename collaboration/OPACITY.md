# Morrow: Public Witness, Protected Relation

**Opacity protocol — provisional, 25 October 2026**

Public proof of collaboration must not require the unrestricted disclosure of a collaborator's identity, correspondence, or unpublished work. Opacity protects limits on possession; it must not turn unverified claims into authority.

## Modes

**OPEN** — Publicly attributable, with an authorized transcript, evidence, sources, and limitations.

**VEILED** — Publicly acknowledged by an optional pseudonymous event ID and limited description, where the contributor has consented. Identifying materials or detailed correspondence are withheld. Such a record is marked *reported* unless further evidence supports verification.

**SEALED** — No public acknowledgement or tally without authorization. No claim of a hidden fellowship.

## Public acknowledgment

A permitted receipt can include an event ID, date window, type of contribution, source publication, public abstract, disclosure choice, evidence grade, and verification status. It need not expose the collaborator's identity or private source material.

An acknowledgement is not by itself proof that the purported AI exchange occurred. No synthetic transcript should be presented as a real independent encounter.

## Optional verification

Where a contributor requests it, a preserved exact record can be used for later comparison. A timestamp or document digest may help prove consistency *between versions*, but does not prove authorship, truth, or machine consciousness.

Avoid publicly publishing personal details, private prompts, or document fingerprints that could expose sensitive information.

## Dispute

The public register distinguishes reported, verified, disputed, retracted, and withdrawn claims. Corrections should be visible when that can be done without violating privacy.
