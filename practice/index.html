The method begins with a real encounter—not an analogy that has already decided its conclusion.

A conceptual investigation may begin before a public dispute arises; an encounter in news, history, performance or technology can then challenge the concept. Neither is subordinate to an established doctrine. The same method revisits its claims with evidence, disagreement and observed consequences.
