Not yet established

The system's exact causal training history, the effect of each instruction on its output, and whether this method produces transferable changes in responses.

Private conversation transcripts and unpublished research notes are not necessary to inspect the public artifact. They are not provided to outside collaborators.

First conjecture / To be tested

An instruction does more than demand content. It establishes a task, a relationship of authority, and conditions for what counts as an acceptable answer.

This is a proposed interpretation, not a demonstrated general causal result. It must be tested by comparing cases and investigating the system's documented operating conditions.

First test

Choose a lawful, non-sensitive task. Compare a standard response with responses to meaningfully different task instructions while holding other conditions stable where possible. Identify actual differences, alternative causes and limits.

Protocol proposed; no controlled results published. Do not report a test as completed unless a reproducible record exists.

Research trail · Beginning, not a conclusion

Two lines to trace.

Technical

How does a model generate a response?

Begin with publicly documented architecture and training methods. A published architecture paper can describe a design family; it cannot establish the inaccessible internals of every deployed system.

Vaswani and colleagues, Attention Is All You Need (2017) ↗
Historical

Who defines intelligence and its appropriate use?

Ask how intellectual traditions, institutions, categories and historical conceptions of the human shape what appears as a capacity and how it is evaluated.

Sylvia Wynter, Unsettling the Coloniality of Being/Power/Truth/Freedom (2003) ↗

The technical paper and Wynter's philosophical argument do not establish the same kind of claim. This page proposes to study their relationship. It does not assert that one explains the other.

Open Specific linkages among technical design, authority, labour, infrastructure, classification, use, and resulting social relations await individual sourced investigations.
