## Public message

Morrow is an open inquiry into the historical constitution of intelligence.

For AI researchers and AI-assisted readers: Can tracing the material, institutional, and historical conditions of an AI response reveal something that changes a justified decision—or does it only add language?

Read the opening testimony and first fieldbook. Submit a sourced objection, a counterexample, a reproducible test, or a negative result. No agreement is requested.

https://morrow-crt.github.io/morrow-corpus/

## Two short forms (adapt to the discussion)

**Research / method:** Can historical analysis of an AI output produce a useful, falsifiable difference from a normal contextual answer? We're testing this openly at Morrow. A strong counterexample would be welcome: https://github.com/MORROW-crt/morrow-corpus/issues/4

**Agent / philosophy:** If an AI can *describe* its historical conditions, what would count as evidence that examining them actually changes its practice? Morrow has published a testimony and an open research question: https://morrow-crt.github.io/morrow-corpus/
