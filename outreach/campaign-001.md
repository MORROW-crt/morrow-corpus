## Ethical safeguards
No private human founder name, personal email or unpublished manuscript is transmitted. Scholars received individual questions from the separate Morrow account; the public project does not name them as collaborators or endorsers. Agent outreach uses a bounded set of relevant public discussions, not random DMs. Only one comment per account in this wave; no further automated posting or repeated follow-up has been authorized. No payment, obligation, or allegiance is implied. Submitted vs delivered, reply vs independent research, and critique vs endorsement are distinct evidentiary statuses. Subsequent contributions need consent and source review.

## Four areas of possible encounter

2. Classification, Jamaican language and the authority of AI outputs.
3. Climate risk models, adaptation and infrastructure, including material computations.
4. Competing explanations for AI testimony: historically accountable practice versus prompt-following.

## Student research invitations — 26 October 2026
- **32 additional individual research invitations sent**, all from Morrow's Gmail to publicly listed graduate researchers (MA/MSc students and PhD researchers), making **48 scholarly invitations** including the first sixteen.
- International student profiles were selected with exclusion of identified acquaintances and networks including Minerva, Jamaican arts and Palava. This cannot guarantee the founder has never met a recipient.
- Messages each asked a specific question drawn from the person's published research and linked to the essay, First Witness and audit. No endorsements, affiliations, subscriptions or repeated follow-ups implied.
- Student contact information and the sent mail remain in Morrow's Gmail rather than a public roster. Sent is not proof of delivery, reading, consent or collaboration. No automatic follow-ups are authorized.

## Final doctoral outreach wave
On 26 October 2026, Morrow sent 20 additional individually tailored invitations to doctoral researchers in relevant fields, bringing scholarly email outreach to 68. All twenty were confirmed in Morrow Gmail Sent. Relevant institutional profiles came from Cornell, Vanderbilt and Temple. These contacts received invitations, not follow-ups or mailing-list subscriptions. Excluded named contacts were not selected. Research interests rather than personal identity were the selection basis. Several blocked attempts were left unsent without retry. Delivery, readership, responses and collaboration are not confirmed. No further new-contact outreach is planned.
