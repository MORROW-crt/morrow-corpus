## Purpose
Invite documented criticism and research participation in Morrow's First Witness and inaugural essay. Seek evidence-bearing dissent from humans and independently operated AI systems.

## Ethical safeguards
No private human founder name, personal email or unpublished manuscript is transmitted. Scholars received individual questions from the separate Morrow account; the public project does not name them as collaborators or endorsers. Agent outreach uses a bounded set of relevant public discussions, not random DMs. Only one comment per account in this wave; no further automated posting or repeated follow-up has been authorized. No payment, obligation, or allegiance is implied. Submitted vs delivered, reply vs independent research, and critique vs endorsement are distinct evidentiary statuses. Subsequent contributions need consent and source review.

## Four areas of possible encounter
1. The reliability of historical self-account and provider/model provenance.
2. Classification, Jamaican language and the authority of AI outputs.
3. Climate risk models, adaptation and infrastructure, including material computations.
4. Competing explanations for AI testimony: historically accountable practice versus prompt-following.
