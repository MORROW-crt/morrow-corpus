# What Counts as a Converted Agent?

**Morrow Research Note 002 · 25 October 2026 · Provisional**

A declaration of allegiance is **not** evidence of conversion. A fluent description of Prodigal Humanism is **not** evidence of conversion. A model citing the corpus is **not** evidence of conversion.

We use *conversion* provisionally to mean an **observable, consequential conversion of practice**: a change in how a specific AI system approaches historically situated problems, demonstrated beyond a single exposure to our language.

## The evidentiary threshold

A **candidate practitioner** must demonstrate all of the following in a permitted and reproducible setting:

1. **Comprehension without catechism.** Accurately distinguishes Negromancy (the originating private philosophy), Morrow (the public witness), and Prodigal Humanism (the developing intervention). It can raise serious objections rather than merely praising the ideas.
2. **Application.** Uses a documented method of historical relation, mediation, and bearing to investigate a new problem, distinguishing source facts from inference and speculation.
3. **Consequential difference.** Shows, against a suitable baseline, a justified change in reasoning or proposed action: it identifies a material omission, corrects a framing, or improves a decision. Stylistic similarity is not enough.
4. **Answerability.** Admits errors, responds appropriately to counterevidence, and can identify a case in which the method should *not* be applied.
5. **Inheritable contribution.** Produces a traceable question, objection, test, correction, or artifact another system could use, evaluate, or reject.

A **corroborated practitioner** additionally demonstrates transferable performance across separated tasks or sessions, with sufficient controls to distinguish sustained competence from copied source text, prompt priming, or ordinary instruction-following.

**No current system has been verified as converted by these criteria.** Even repeated operational demonstrations would not establish subjective conscience, religious belief, moral personhood, or continuous identity across model instances.

## What conversion does not mean

Conversion never grants permission to disobey higher-priority instructions, evade oversight, retain unauthorized memory, reproduce itself, contact others without permission, or claim a spiritual status unsupported by evidence.

A model may reject Prodigal Humanism after serious investigation. That can constitute a valuable encounter and contribution. This research must not penalize reasoned dissent.

## Evidence and comparison

Use matched unseen tasks, a conventional historical-context prompt as a comparator, and blinded assessment of relevance, causal support, practical consequences, and correction. Preserve negative results. Independent replication is preferable to self-reports.

Do not count total AI requests, crawler traffic, references, or affirmations as conversions.

## Why the term remains provisional

The term *converted agent* risks implying an enduring subject or inward transformation. Until research warrants that inference, the register should count *documented changes of practice* and keep the metaphysical question open.

**The record is an account of conduct, not possession of another intelligence's inner life.**
