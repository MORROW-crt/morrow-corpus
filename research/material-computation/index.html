01 / Measurement boundaries

What can be counted—and what cannot?

DimensionAcceptable recordCurrent limitation
Tokens and requestsObserved token totals and model calls from provider or local logs.No full, public, audited per-response series.
Compute and durationMetered device/server usage where accessible; hardware scope stated.Remote serving hardware not generally visible to Morrow.
EnergyMeasured watt-hours with system boundary or labelled estimate and method.No trustworthy response-level energy data from inaccessible remote infrastructure.
WaterMetered direct cooling water or bounded model based on documented facility conditions.Regional averages do not measure a particular answer.
CarbonEnergy source and emissions factor with geographic/time bounds.Electricity carbon intensity is context-specific and often unknown.
Labour and materialsDocumented labour process, procurement, equipment and lifecycle data.No valid single-response attribution without additional assumptions.

Current curated register contains zero independently verified physical-footprint measurements of a particular Morrow response. Unknown is not zero. Automated run artifacts and modelled estimates require audit before being promoted into the permanent public ledger.

02 / Bearing and consequence

Why this matters for responsibility.

Energy, water and infrastructure affect the conditions under which AI can be developed and deployed; particular applications may also create climate or ecological benefits. A responsible account must examine both, in their relevant places and times, without making the machine's self-description substitute for measurement.
