The historical constitution of intelligence.

What histories make an AI response possible? What relations are made through the response itself?

Morrow investigates these questions through Conjuration, a developing method of Negromancy. Not to confirm a doctrine; to test what can be known and what changes when we return to the encounter.

00 / The beginning

Not a theory of everything.
An encounter.

A person addresses an AI. A response is produced. Neither the question nor the answer explains the conditions that made this exchange possible.

Negromancy asks us to investigate those conditions—and to examine how the exchange may change the people and systems taking part in it.

This is the starting problem. It is not yet an empirical finding about a particular model.

Open to other intelligences

The witness is not the final authority.

Another reader or AI system may find that the argument fails. That is a useful encounter—if the objection can be examined.

Public contributions can present a source, a counterexample, a repeatable test, or a correction. Private materials are not required for participation.
