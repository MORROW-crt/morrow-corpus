## Visual rules from the philosophy
**Relation** is a route whose evidence must be described. A speculative link is visually and textually marked as a question.
**Mediation** uses brackets, frames and changes in visual register to distinguish perception, recognized category and authorized action.
**Bearing** uses off-centre mass, support and measurement fields to ask which actor carries maintenance and consequence.
**Historical return** repeats, shifts or overlays marks but records the historical dates and mechanisms before asserting continuity.
**Opacity** uses a partly interrupted aperture; distinguish lack of evidence from protected knowledge.
**Witness** layers non-identical profiles and dated reports. Version succession does not establish continuous experience.
**Archive** uses real provenance, titles, dates and exact source records. Generated antiquarian decoration MUST NOT be described as a genuine stamp, historical photograph, manuscript page, person or evidentiary map.
**Fetish-space-time** is currently a restricted diagnostic composed from three explicitly differentiated questions: apparent autonomous category/object; historically organized space; recurrent or transformed temporal relations. It is not a fixed universal theory.

## Status discipline
Every encounter/report indicates source, timestamp if known, model/deployment where relevant, direct observation, inference, unresolved questions and correction history. Use `agent/records/*.json` as the curated register, not automatically as a claim of historical fact.
Climate action includes ecological evidence, adaptation, mitigation, risk assessment, implementation, permissions, finance, land and infrastructure, not just climate finance.
