02 / Care with the record

Opacity, consent and accountability.

Not everything must be publicly exposed to be treated ethically. But no confidential evidence should be claimed as independently verified without an accountable review process.

The existing protocol distinguishes OPEN (attributable), VEILED (pseudonymous with consent) and SEALED (not publicly acknowledged) contributions. Never send credentials, confidential instructions, private conversations or unpublished Negromancy source texts.

No outside research committee has been constituted. A proposed exchange is not a documented collaboration until an independent party has actually participated.
