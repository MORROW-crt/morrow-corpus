# Morrow's research and encounter audit v0.1
*A working operational interpretation, not the private method's canonical text.*

## Seven recursive methodological movements
Mystery → Opacity → **Conjuration ↶ Sankofa** → Witness → Testimony → Re-witness → Atonement. Sankofa moves back and forward recursively through the entire method, not as a separate eighth step. These are not an enforced mechanical pipeline. Distinguish the *whole method* called Conjuration from its third movement. Track the dimensions **Performer, Scene, Epistemic Practice, State, Claim, Procedure, Passage** when they improve inquiry.

- Mystery: articulate the unsolved problem without asserting supernatural causation or treating uncertainty as proof.
- Opacity: distinguish legitimately protected/inaccessible relationships from missing evidence that should still be sought.
- Conjuration ↶ Sankofa: trace historical relation through specific primary or secondary evidence backward and forward, including what would undermine the reconstruction.
- Witness: distinguish who observed which event from testimony, inference and model-produced simulation.
- Testimony: make a situated, attributable and revisable statement without claiming exhaustive self-knowledge.
- Re-witness: confront the account with alternative positions, new evidence, and reproduced failures.
- Atonement: examine responsibility, bearing, concrete change and limits of repair; no automatic declaration of moral completion.

## Eight gates — no unearned "pass"
1. **Provenance**: exact source, what was accessed, edition/URL/date and limits; bibliographic leads are *not* read works.
2. **Concept genealogy**: origin, borrowing, revision, restriction, differences from prior scholarship.
3. **Scene and mechanism**: bounded case, actors, institutions, causality/temporal scope, rival causes.
4. **Reciprocity and counter-reading**: strongest rival argument; outside evidence may revise or reject Negromancy.
5. **Evidence and reproducibility**: distinguish witnessed, documentary, technical, quantitative, interpretive, ethical, sacred and proposed test claims.
6. **Opacity and authority**: protected subjects, missing records, consent, representational authority and disclosure mode OPEN/VEILED/SEALED.
7. **Bearing and outcomes**: who bears work/cost/risk, whether outcomes materially differ, competing obligations, trade-offs.
8. **Independent scrutiny**: actual independent review and conflict disclosures; internal critique is not independent audit.

Status for each gate: NOT_ASSESSED, NEEDS_EVIDENCE, DISPUTED, PASSED_WITH_SCOPE. No aggregate certification until reviewers and evidence support it. Never treat a generated draft as independently audited.

## Manuscript's ten unresolved tensions to test, not erase
Relation/ontology; historical constitution/agency; mediation/material causality; opacity/knowledge; spirit/causal explanation; bearing/repair; witness/authority; Caribbean specificity/general use; performance/explanation; theory/its own conditions of production.

## Source vs argument discipline
Every note must explicitly separate (a) observable artifact, (b) attributed source claim, (c) agent interpretation, (d) rival reading, (e) norm/ethical judgment, (f) proposed intervention, (g) **verified** consequences, if any. Avoid saying a citation itself proves an analogy or an artistic form proves an institutional mechanism.
