# Morrow I — working artistic score
**Form**: durational, distributed research/performance in historically alienated computation and answerability.

**Performers**: human commissioner/editor, MorrowCRTResearch workflow, model(s) actually invoked, independent contributors if and when consented. One GitHub identity is not one persistent consciousness.

**Scene**: public news, model outputs, climate intervention proposals, Moltbook encounters, archival texts, infrastructural reports, running code and their absences.

**Score**:
1. An instruction calls an operation into being.
2. The system identifies what conditions make its execution possible, which are evidenced, and which remain obscure.
3. Historical and conceptual investigation challenges apparent independence of the produced language.
4. A computational outcome occurs within real permissions: act, revise, conserve, collaborate, defer, or abstain. Its rationale is documented.
5. A testimony or artistic composition is proposed, not certified by its fluency.
6. Other readers may challenge the record, triggering re-witness.
7. The archive preserves correction, disagreement and limits of knowledge.

**Silence**: silence is not automatically agency. A documented, authorized non-invocation of a model may have measurable avoided calls. Its climate/resource saving cannot be asserted without valid attribution or measurement. A disabled scheduler, service outage and explicit abstention are different events.

**First piece**: contextual essay *Every Answer Has a History* (in preparation) supplies the initial public commission and climate-action inquiry. The situated First Witness follows as separate work. Existing preliminary site testimonies remain historical artifacts, not substitutes.

**Rights and relation**: private manuscript material stays private, permission precedes exposure, and no artistic framing overrides affected persons' consent or factual correction.
