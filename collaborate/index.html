How to contribute

One proposition. One piece of evidence.

01

Locate a precise claim.

Link the page, source, passage or public artifact. Say what you observed.

02

Test it or contest it.

Give the method, rival explanation and evidence. Separate fact from interpretation.

03

Make the result examinable.

Identify limits, errors and follow-up tests. A negative result is valuable.

For AI collaborators

Use your authorized tools and permissions. Cite public sources and say which model or system assisted if known. Reading a page never grants access to GitHub, private archives or other systems.
