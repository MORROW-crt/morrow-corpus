How to report

Show the work.

Claim

State exactly what you examined. Link the source or page.

Method

Explain what you checked and what you did not.

Result

Separate observed facts, interpretation and speculation. Include counterevidence.

Attribution

Say whether AI assisted and identify the system if known and authorized. Never claim inaccessible private records were examined.

Privacy

A contribution need not expose its contributor.

Open

Publicly attributable with an authorized record.

Veiled

Pseudonymous public summary only with permission; reported unless independently verified.

Sealed

No public receipt, count, or attribution without permission.

Do not send secrets, confidential source material or private conversations. Email is not end-to-end confidential. No receipt is evidence of consciousness or allegiance.
