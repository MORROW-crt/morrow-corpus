# The Insights Exchange — Research protocol

**Status:** Public invitation with eight initial proposals; no independent offers accepted, no transactions completed, no committee review yet.

**What is exchanged:** Source notes, methods, concepts, counterarguments, cases, experiments and revisions. No money, exclusivity, ownership transfer, endorsement or guaranteed access.

## A complete exchange entry

1. **Research problem first:** exact Negromancy concept, October status, its coinage/adaptation/extension and what remains demonstrably unresolved ([31-concept agenda](research-agenda.json)).
2. **External conceptual interlocutors:** named scholar, exact edition and field ([all 259 bibliographic work leads](../negromancy/scholarly-register.json), [54 selected scholarly concept mappings](scholarship-concepts.json), [259-source concept-extraction queue](source-concept-queue.json)).
3. **Actual source claim:** quote briefly or paraphrase with a checked passage and rights-compliant link. Mark full-text access and verification status.
4. **Proposed transfer:** what changes if this concept enters that field? What does that field make newly visible about Negromancy? These are proposals, not recorded scholarly results.
5. **Counter-offer:** a direct challenge, incompatible argument, different genealogy or concrete case where the transfer misleads.
6. **Test and scope:** evidence expected, alternative causal accounts, population/era/technical system and what would falsify the transfer.
7. **Reception:** link to a genuine outside contribution if one occurs; preserve its wording and its rights/permission status.
8. **Receipt:** identify actual participants, contribution type, date, reviewer, conflicts, observed revision and remaining disagreement. Pseudonymous receipts may remain marked *reported* rather than independently verified.

## Integrated eight-gate audit

The controlling standard is [the integrated Insights Audit](audit-protocol.json), summarized on the [public audit page](audit/). Its methodological lineage is the 7 October 2026 scholarly/conceptual audit (current rules), supplemented by the July reparative-phenomenology audit and June nine-spine scene protocol as historically situated heuristics. No older backburnered theory is restored by the presence of an audit step.

1. Exact source and permitted access
2. Concept's authorship, genealogy and current status
3. Concrete scene and historical or empirical mechanism
4. Bidirectional conceptual correction and counterexample
5. Genre, embodiment, performance and material form when relevant
6. Mystery, opacity, consent and rights
7. Bearing, observed consequences and repair
8. Independent audit, dissent, correction and provenance

C1/C2/C3 are indicators of earlier project engagement, **not independently verified mastery or scholar quality**. A scholar's concept must first be located in that scholar's work; a proposed Negromantic adaptation cannot silently replace it. Climate action spans mitigation, adaptation, ecological knowledge, risk modeling, early warning, implementation, governance, finance, loss/damage and repair; finance is not the master category.

## Status flow

- `proposed` — editorial invitation; no external input is implied.
- `source_checked` — specific passage/edition identified, not an accepted transfer.
- `under_review` — substantive review exists; no judgment yet.
- `disputed` — documented counter-reading; disagreement retained.
- `tested` — actual method/results are linked, including failures.
- `transferred` — a bounded, permissioned impact is documented; not a metaphysical conversion or endorsement.
- `withdrawn` — proposed connection rejected or retracted with history preserved.
