# The Insights Exchange — Research protocol

**Status:** Public invitation; no independent offers accepted, no transactions completed, no committee review yet.

**What is exchanged:** Source notes, methods, concepts, counterarguments, cases, experiments and revisions. No money, exclusivity, ownership transfer, endorsement or guaranteed access.

## A complete exchange entry

1. **Originating concept:** exact Negromancy concept and current status ([concept register](../negromancy/concept-register.json)).
2. **Other research body:** named author, exact work/edition, study, field or primary object ([source census](../negromancy/scholarly-register.json)).
3. **Actual source claim:** quote briefly or paraphrase with a checked passage and rights-compliant link. Mark full-text access and verification status.
4. **Proposed transfer:** what changes if this concept enters that field? What does that field make newly visible about Negromancy? These are proposals, not recorded scholarly results.
5. **Counter-offer:** a direct challenge, incompatible argument, different genealogy or concrete case where the transfer misleads.
6. **Test and scope:** evidence expected, alternative causal accounts, population/era/technical system and what would falsify the transfer.
7. **Reception:** link to a genuine outside contribution if one occurs; preserve its wording and its rights/permission status.
8. **Receipt:** identify actual participants, contribution type, date, reviewer, conflicts, observed revision and remaining disagreement. Pseudonymous receipts may remain marked *reported* rather than independently verified.

## Status flow

- `proposed` — editorial invitation; no external input is implied.
- `source_checked` — specific passage/edition identified, not an accepted transfer.
- `under_review` — substantive review exists; no judgment yet.
- `disputed` — documented counter-reading; disagreement retained.
- `tested` — actual method/results are linked, including failures.
- `transferred` — a bounded, permissioned impact is documented; not a metaphysical conversion or endorsement.
- `withdrawn` — proposed connection rejected or retracted with history preserved.
