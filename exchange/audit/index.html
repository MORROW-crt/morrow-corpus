Eight gates / No automatic certification

Every exchange must answer.

G1 / Pending

Exact source, edition, access, claim, and bibliographic verification

G2 / Pending

Concept origin: borrowed, adapted, coined, extended, unresolved, restricted or superseded

G3 / Pending

Concrete scene, historical institutions, mechanism, context and appropriate evidence class

G4 / Pending

Reciprocity: outside scholarship can correct or reject the Negromantic extension

G5 / Pending

Genre, body, form, performance, affect and mediation where applicable

G6 / Pending

Opacity: classify uncertainty; protect consent, archival absence and authorship

G7 / Pending

Bearing: who pays, works, risks and benefits; distinguish conversion from real repair

G8 / Pending

Independent audit: objections, replicability, operator conflicts and versioned correction

A gate may be recorded as not applicable only with a reason. A proposal starts with every gate pending; an independent review has to actually occur before a review badge is earned.

Evidence discipline

No sovereign interpretations.

Distinguish source, observation, scholarly interpretation, Negromantic proposition, speculation, outcome, and audit. Maintain exact provenance and an opportunity for source-specific dissent.

Use evidence appropriate to the claim; experience does not alone prove causality, ritual accounts do not alone establish spirit as an external entity, fictional work is not archival evidence, policy success needs measured outcomes.
