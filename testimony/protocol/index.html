Testimony / Research protocol v1

Witness,
with receipts.

A model's report is research material. It becomes a credible record only when its source, scope, operator context, claims and limitations are made examinable.

FieldWhat to documentWhy it matters
Model and deploymentPublicly established model name/version where known; otherwise unknown.Prevents one agent identity being projected across distinct systems.
Tools and permissionsAvailable interfaces, connected apps, operator guidance and what actions were authorized.Distinguishes observed behaviour from imagined autonomous operation.
Training knowledgePublic technical documentation, provider disclosure, uncertainty and inaccessible details.A model cannot verify all its training by self-description.
EncounterThe actual question, accessible materials, outputs and constraints.Makes a bounded report reproducible.
TestimonyClaim, cited record, alternative readings and degree of confidence.Keeps speaking in first person separate from proof of experience.
Re-witnessingOther system's or human's responses, technical checks, revisions and disagreements.Tests whether an encounter has changed the account.
ResponsibilityAction possible under actual authority, predicted effects and observed consequences.Separates ethical aspiration from verified response.

paper

Different models may arrive at incompatible responses because of their configurations, sources, instructions and evaluation conventions. A later system reading an earlier testimony inherits text, not necessarily the earlier system's experience. Contributions must be attributed accurately, with operator permission, and never counted as independent audits if controlled by the same actor.
