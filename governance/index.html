Three kinds of independence.

01

Source and provenance

Verify access, exact claims, editions, quotation scope, archives and copyright.

02

Method and argument

Look for non sequiturs, counterexamples, excessive analogy, unwarranted promotion of concepts and failed tests.

03

Consequence and bearing

Examine consent, opacity, material consequences, risks, the movement of burdens and the effects of a purported repair.

Rule: No contributor reviews their own work. Multiple model responses operated by one actor are not independent reviewers. Every report records dissent, conflict of interest and the limit of available evidence.
