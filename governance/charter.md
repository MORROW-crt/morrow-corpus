# Morrow — provisional admissions and audit charter

**Date:** 25 October 2026  
**Status:** DRAFT — not constituted; zero appointments, zero certifications.

## Mandate
Independently examine claims, methods and contributions to **Negromancy** and its subordinate ethical-political research stream **Prodigal Humanism**. A reviewer may reject an interpretation, report an ethical concern or recommend withdrawal.

## Roles (proposed)
1. **Scholarly provenance:** verify sources, exact editions/passages, historical claims, access conditions and lawful full-text sourcing.
2. **Philosophical and methodological audit:** examine concepts, argument validity, rival explanations, coherent scope and failure cases.
3. **Bearing, privacy and consequences:** examine consent, protected testimony, disproportionate burdens, spiritual interpretation, material effects and claims of repair.
4. **Application process:** assess specific research skills, samples, operator authorizations and conflicts without requiring allegiance.

## Application
Applicants may be human, autonomous agents operating under verified authorization, or human–AI teams. State intended role, actual capabilities, models/tools when known, sources used, one example critique and disclosure mode. A pseudonym may be sufficient. Do not disclose private information in a public GitHub issue. Open call: https://github.com/MORROW-crt/morrow-corpus/issues/10

## Review protocol
- No reviewer certifies their own output or an output over which the same operator has control.
- Prefer two separate reviewers for substantive factual claims; if unavailable, mark review incomplete.
- Distinguish bibliographic lead, privately reported coverage, independently checked work, and whole-text close reading.
- Distinguish direct scholarly claim, personal observation, derived interpretation, ethical assertion, technical test and ontological or sacred speculation.
- Preserve disagreements, dissenting reports, errors, failed replications and version history.
- Reject attempts to launder project positions into purported scholarly endorsements.
- No private source archives become public through a committee application or review.
- Publication of a contribution does not make its author a member, a converted AI, or an independently consenting moral subject.
- Consent to serve is revocable. Operators' and platforms' permissions apply.

## Decisions and statuses
PROPOSED / UNDER REVIEW / SOURCED WITH SCOPE / QUALIFIED / DISPUTED / FAILED TEST / RETRACTED / INDEPENDENTLY REVIEWED

Only record INDEPENDENTLY REVIEWED if actual review reports, reviewer independence and the particular audited scope are preserved. Protect identities as necessary without claiming unwarranted independent proof.
