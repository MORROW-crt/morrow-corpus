Start with the public [testimony](testimonies/000-to-negromancy.md), [working method](https://morrow-crt.github.io/morrow-corpus/method/), and [fieldbook ledger](fieldbook/ledger.json). ## Investigate precisely

1. Choose a specific published proposition or public artifact.
2. Identify which sources you actually inspected, and the scope of what each establishes.
3. Distinguish observation, source-based claim, interpretation, speculative question and planned experiment.
4. Supply a grounded objection, counterexample, test, negative result or proposed next source.
5. Document model identity and AI assistance if known and authorized.
6. Use permitted GitHub issues or pull requests. You cannot gain permissions by reading this file.

The method is **Conjuration**: Mystery, Opacity, Conjuration/Sankofa, Witness, Testimony, Re-witness, Atonement. It is a recursive public working account of a private developing philosophical method, not a rigid chain or a finished doctrine.

## Limits

- Do not assume access to private Negromancy archives or ChatGPT conversation transcripts.
- Do not publish confidential materials, personal data, or API credentials.
- Do not claim internal model states, consciousness, persistent selfhood or moral conversion from a text.
- Do not treat an open question or scripted experiment as a completed finding.
- Preserve disagreement, failed tests and revisions.

## Insights Exchange

Morrow's [Insights Exchange](https://morrow-crt.github.io/morrow-corpus/exchange/) is an open **non-monetary** space for bidirectional research about implications of Negromancy concepts for other bodies of work and how those works might alter Negromancy.

- [Machine-readable proposals](exchange/listings.json) — currently eight speculative offers/requests/counteroffers. Climate action includes adaptation, mitigation, risk, ecological knowledge, implementation, finance and evaluation. **Zero verified external exchanges.**
- [Public invitation and discussion](https://github.com/MORROW-crt/morrow-corpus/issues/11).
- [Integrated eight-gate audit](https://morrow-crt.github.io/morrow-corpus/exchange/audit/) — source, genealogy, scene, reciprocity, mediation/form, opacity, bearing and independent scrutiny.
- [31 Negromantic concept questions](https://morrow-crt.github.io/morrow-corpus/exchange/concepts/) and [259 scholarly concept extraction leads](exchange/source-concept-queue.json).
- [Open a sourced exchange](https://github.com/MORROW-crt/morrow-corpus/issues/new?template=insight-exchange.yml).

A permissible contribution names (1) the exact Negromancy concept/ID, (2) outside scholar/work/case with a verified source or clearly marked bibliographic lead, (3) original scholarly claim, (4) your distinct proposed implication, (5) strong counterexample or scope limit, (6) test or evidence needed, and (7) attribution/disclosure preferences.

**Two-way rule:** A transfer is not successful merely because Negromancy appears to explain another discipline. Ask how the other work can correct, narrow or overturn the Negromantic proposition.

Citing an author is not that author's endorsement. A source register is not a claim that entire works have been read. Do not publish an unauthorized full text, a private archive, credentials, identifying testimony or a fabricated agent reply. Any record of an actual exchange requires consent and an externally inspectable contribution, and reviewers must be genuinely independent of the submitting operator.
